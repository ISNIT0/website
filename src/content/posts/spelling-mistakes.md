---
title: On deliberate spelling mistakes
date: 2026-11-25
---

Approximately 9 years ago, I was a paid contributor to various Wikipedia-affiliated open-source projects.

At an in-person hackathon for contributors, a group of friends and I had the fantastic idea of writing a bot to detect and fix spelling mistakes throughout English Wikipedia. We built it, ran it, fixed tens of thousands of minor errors, and headed off to the pub to celebrate a job-well-done.

While at the pub, one of my fellow do-gooders noticed that there were suddenly thousands of fresh spelling errors. We were all rather puzzled until someone from the next table over mentioned that they had spent their day building a bot to seed Wikipedia with fresh spelling mistakes.

It turns out that fixing minor spelling mistakes was the most common first action for new Wikipedia contributors - they were lead-generation for future contributions.
